The Light That Leads My Journey

Being a student comes with a lot of pressure, late-night studying, and moments where I really doubt myself. But whenever things get overwhelming, my faith, my family, and my friends serve as a guiding light that keeps me moving forward. That light reminds me of my purpose, gives me hope, and inspires me to be a positive influence to others on campus. Ultimately, it doesn't just show me which path to take—it shapes the kind of person I am becoming every single day.
